The Process

From quiz to career in four steps.

Pathfinder turns self-discovery into a clear, actionable roadmap — no guesswork, no overwhelm.

01
Take the Career Quiz
Answer 10 questions about your skills, interests, personality and values. It takes about 5 minutes and there are no right or wrong answers — just honest ones.
02
Get Your Career Matches
Instantly receive a ranked list of careers best suited to you, each with a compatibility score, salary overview, day-to-day description, and growth potential.
03
Explore Paths & Requirements
Pick a career and explore which universities and employers are right for you. See every skill and qualification required, and exactly how many you already have.
04
Learn, Track & Achieve
Follow personalised learning roadmaps, build skills daily with our tracker, and stay motivated with streaks and milestone badges along the way.
