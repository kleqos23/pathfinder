Our Story

Built by students, for students.

We know the confusion of choosing a career path. Pathfinder was born to solve exactly that.

We're a team of students who have been there — staring at a list of university courses with no idea which one to choose. The career advice we got was either too generic or too overwhelming. So we decided to build something better.

Pathfinder was created as part of our ITP project, but it addresses a real and universal problem: too many students choose careers without knowing if those careers actually suit them, or what it takes to get there.

We believe every student deserves a clear, personalised map to their future — not generic advice, but a data-driven roadmap that grows with them as they learn, develop skills, and discover what they love.
